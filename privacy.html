ScanSign — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

ScanSign ("the app") is a document scanner and PDF signing app published by Usman ("we"). This policy explains what information the app uses and why.

Summary

  • Your documents and signatures are processed on your phone. We (the developer) never receive them.
  • Google sign-in is optional. If you sign in, your files are copied to your own Google Drive.
  • The app shows ads from Google AdMob, which may use your device's advertising ID.

Documents, photos and signatures

Documents you scan or open, photos you pick, and signatures you draw or capture are processed on your phone and saved in the app's private storage. A file leaves your phone only when you choose to share it, save it to Downloads, or back it up to Google Drive (below).

Optional Google sign-in and Drive backup

If you choose "Sign in with Google", the app receives your Google account name and email address to show which account is signed in, and asks for permission to access only the files it creates in your Google Drive (the drive.file permission). Your documents and signatures are then uploaded over an encrypted connection to a "ScanSign" folder in your Google Drive so you can restore them on another phone. These files are stored by Google under your account and Google's privacy policy; we do not have access to them.

You can sign out at any time in the app (Account & backup). To remove the backup, delete the "ScanSign" folder in Google Drive, and you can remove the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Advertising

The app shows banner ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from it, and how you interact with ads. In the European Economic Area and the UK you are asked for consent first, and you can change your choice at any time under Account & backup → "Privacy & ad choices". You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

See how Google uses this information: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and Google's privacy policy policies.google.com/privacy.

Permissions

  • Internet — for Google sign-in, Drive backup and ads.
  • Camera / photos — used through Android's own camera and photo picker, only when you tap to take or choose a picture.
  • Storage (Android 9 and older only) — to save PDFs to your Downloads folder when you ask.

Deleting your data

Delete any document or signature inside the app; if you are signed in, its Drive copy is moved to your Drive trash. Uninstalling the app removes everything stored on the phone.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
