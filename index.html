ScanSign

Scan. Sign. Share.

ScanSign is an Android app that turns paper into clean PDFs and lets you sign them right on your phone.

Features

  • Scan documents with the camera and save them as PDF.
  • Open any PDF and add your signature, then share or save it.
  • Keep saved signatures ready to reuse.
  • Optional Google sign-in to back up your files to your own Google Drive (folder "ScanSign"). The app can only see the files it creates.

Your privacy

Your documents are processed on your phone. We never receive them. Read the full privacy policy.
